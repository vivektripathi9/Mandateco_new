About

We Are in the Business of Building Sales.

MANDATECO is a real estate Sales as a Service company created to help developers build stronger, more accountable and scalable sales operations.

Founder

Karamala Pradeep

Founder, MANDATECO

His Philosophy

“The right strategy, the right people, and the right processes enabled with technology can turn project potential into measurable sales.”

Karamala Pradeep is a real estate sales professional with extensive experience in residential sales, project mandates, sales strategy and business development. He founded Go-Digital in 2020, focusing on residential sales and mandate-led opportunities.

With a strong understanding of market dynamics and customer behaviour, he founded MANDATECO with a vision to build structured, scalable and performance-driven sales ecosystems for real estate developers.

His philosophy is simple: the right strategy, people and processes enabled with technology can transform project potential into measurable sales.

Our Philosophy

Sales Is Not an Activity. It Is a System.

Generating enquiries is only one part of selling real estate. The real opportunity lies in what happens next.

Within the hour. Speed protects intent before interest cools.

Against budget, timeline, need and fit - not against a script.

With timely follow-ups that keep the project in the conversation.

Only when the buyer is ready. Visits are booked to convert, not fill a calendar.

Objections are logged, next steps owned, and the deal stays warm.

With clear pricing logic, controlled concessions and one accountable owner.

Discipline across every stage - not a last-minute push.
