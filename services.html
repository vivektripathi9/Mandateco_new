Services

End-to-End Sales. One Integrated Partner.

MANDATECO provides developers with an integrated sales ecosystem covering the complete customer journey - from first strategy to final booking.

01

Sales Strategy & Planning

Start With the Right Strategy.

Every project has a different product, market, customer and commercial objective. A generic sales plan leaves inventory stuck and teams guessing.

We develop project-specific sales strategies that define who we sell to, how we sell, and what success looks like before the first pitch goes out.

  • Project positioning
  • Product proposition
  • Target audience
  • Pricing
  • Competition
  • Market dynamics
  • Sales objectives
  • Inventory and sales velocity

02

Sales Team as a Service

The Right People. Deployed for Your Project.

We build and deploy dedicated sales teams aligned with the project’s objectives - not a rotating desk of freelancers.

The team is briefed on product, pricing and buyer profile, then owned against the numbers that move inventory.

  • Inside sales
  • Lead qualification
  • Customer engagement
  • Site sales
  • Follow-ups
  • Negotiations
  • Closure management

03

Lead Management

Every Lead Deserves a Process.

Enquiries lose value when response is slow, qualification is vague, or follow-up depends on memory.

We create structured lead-management systems that help teams respond faster, qualify better and follow up consistently - so every enquiry has a next step.

  1. Enquiry
  2. Qualification
  3. Engagement
  4. Follow-up
  5. Site Visit
  6. Negotiation
  7. Closure

04

Site Visit Management

Turn Interest Into Experience.

The site visit is one of the most important moments in the real estate customer journey. It is where interest becomes conviction - or walks away.

We design the visit as a conversion moment: prepared buyers, a clear product story, and disciplined follow-up after they leave the site.

  • Visit scheduling
  • Customer preparation
  • Site experience
  • Sales presentation
  • Objection handling
  • Post-visit follow-up
  • Conversion

05

CRM & Sales Operations

Bring Visibility to the Funnel.

Without a single view of the pipeline, developers see activity - not outcomes. Teams chase noise instead of stuck deals.

We integrate CRM and reporting systems so lead source, stage, owner and conversion are visible from enquiry to closure.

  • Pipeline visibility
  • Stage ownership
  • Source tracking
  • Conversion reporting
  • Weekly performance reviews
  • Actionable dashboards

06

Closure Management

Because the Last Mile Matters.

Sales opportunities can be lost even after strong customer engagement - when negotiation drifts, paperwork stalls, or no one owns the booking.

Our closure-focused approach brings structure to the final stretch so intent becomes a confirmed sale.

  • Negotiation
  • Follow-up
  • Objection handling
  • Commercial discussions
  • Documentation coordination
  • Booking conversion

CRM & Analytics
