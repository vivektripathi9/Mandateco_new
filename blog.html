Mandate

A project needs a sales system

Launch is not the finish line. Brochures, pricing and a site office get the project into the market - but inventory only moves when someone owns what happens after the enquiry lands.

A sales system defines who responds, how leads are qualified, when visits are booked, and who closes. Without it, activity looks busy and the pipeline stays soft.

MANDATECO builds that system around the project: strategy, people, process and accountability under one mandate.

Floor

The site visit is the sale

A walkthrough is a tour. A visit closes when the floor knows the product, the buyer’s objections, and the next step before anyone leaves the site.

Scheduling, preparation, presentation and post-visit follow-up are not extras - they are the conversion path. When visits are treated as calendar fillers, intent cools overnight.

We design site visits as conversion moments: prepared buyers, a clear product story, and disciplined ownership after the visit.

Closure

The handshake is not the close

Strong engagement can still fail at the last mile - when negotiation drifts, paperwork stalls, or no one owns the booking.

Token, documentation and the final objection stay with the same mandate that brought the buyer in. Handoffs at this stage are where deals leak.

Closure management brings structure to commercial discussions, follow-up and booking conversion so intent becomes a confirmed sale.
