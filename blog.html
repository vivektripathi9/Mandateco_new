Land

Why Invest in Farm Plots? A Smart Way to Own Land Beyond the City

As cities continue to expand, many investors are looking beyond conventional apartments and commercial properties and exploring farm plots and managed land developments. Farm plots offer a combination of land ownership, lifestyle opportunities and long-term potential, making them an interesting option for people looking to diversify their real-estate portfolio.

01

Ownership of Land

Land is a finite resource. Unlike many constructed properties, where the structure depreciates over time, the underlying land remains the core asset.

Investing in a farm plot gives you ownership of a tangible piece of land that can potentially serve multiple purposes, subject to local regulations and the project’s approved usage.

02

Growing Preference for Nature-Led Living

The demand for spaces away from the noise and density of the city has increased as people look for healthier and more relaxed weekend lifestyles.

Farm developments can provide an opportunity to enjoy:

  • Open green spaces
  • Fresh air and natural surroundings
  • Private outdoor spaces
  • Weekend retreats
  • Farming and gardening experiences, where permitted

This makes farm plots relevant not only as an investment but also as a lifestyle asset.

03

Diversification of Your Real-Estate Portfolio

Many investors traditionally focus on apartments, villas or commercial properties. A farm plot can add another category to a real-estate portfolio.

Instead of concentrating investments in one type of property, land ownership can provide a different form of real-estate exposure.

04

Lower Maintenance Compared to Built Properties

A plot without a large constructed structure generally requires less structural maintenance than an apartment or villa.

For buyers who are primarily interested in owning land and developing it according to applicable regulations in the future, this can be an attractive consideration.

05

Potential for Future Appreciation

The value of land is influenced by several factors, including location, connectivity, infrastructure development, surrounding development and demand.

Farm plots located near expanding urban corridors can benefit if the surrounding region experiences sustained development. However, appreciation is never guaranteed and should be evaluated based on the specific location and project.

06

A Weekend Lifestyle Asset

A farm plot can also be more than an investment. With the right development, it can become a place for weekend getaways, family gatherings, gardening, outdoor activities and spending quality time away from the city.

Growth

Why Invest in North Bangalore? Understanding the Growth Story

North Bangalore has emerged as one of the city’s major growth corridors, supported by improving connectivity, employment centres, infrastructure development and its proximity to Kempegowda International Airport. For property investors, the region represents an important part of Bangalore’s expanding urban landscape.

01

Proximity to Kempegowda International Airport

One of North Bangalore’s biggest advantages is its proximity to Kempegowda International Airport. Airport connectivity has contributed to the development of surrounding commercial, residential and hospitality ecosystems, while making the northern part of Bengaluru increasingly accessible to businesses and travellers.

02

Strong Road Connectivity

North Bangalore is connected to several important highways and arterial roads, creating access towards the airport, central Bengaluru and other emerging corridors.

Large-scale road infrastructure such as the Satellite Town Ring Road (STRR) is designed to improve regional connectivity and facilitate movement around Bengaluru rather than requiring all traffic to pass through the city. NHAI documents identify the Bengaluru Ring Road/STRR network as a major highway infrastructure project.

03

Expanding Commercial and Employment Ecosystem

The northern region has seen the development of business parks, logistics facilities, industrial clusters, hospitality projects and residential communities. As employment opportunities and commercial activity expand, the need for housing, retail, services and recreational spaces can also increase.

04

Multiple Growth Corridors

North Bangalore is not dependent on a single neighbourhood. Areas around Yelahanka, Doddaballapura, Devanahalli, Hebbal, Jakkur and surrounding corridors have been witnessing varying levels of infrastructure and real-estate development. This creates a broader ecosystem rather than a single-property-market story.

05

Infrastructure Can Influence Real-Estate Demand

Infrastructure is one of the important factors that buyers consider when evaluating a property. Metro expansion, highways, ring roads and improved access to employment centres can influence how attractive a location becomes over time. Bengaluru’s metro network is continuing to expand, with BMRCL listing upcoming projects and extensions as part of the city’s future transportation network.

06

Scope for Long-Term Land Investment

For investors with a longer investment horizon, emerging areas of North Bangalore can be considered alongside more established parts of the city. The important point is to distinguish between announced infrastructure, under-construction projects and completed infrastructure, and to evaluate each property based on its actual distance and access to these developments.

Corridors

Why Invest in Kanakapura Road, STRR Hoskote & Nearby Growth Corridors?

Bangalore’s expansion is no longer restricted to the traditional city limits. Several peripheral corridors are gradually developing into important residential, industrial, logistics and lifestyle destinations. Among these, Kanakapura Road, the STRR corridor around Hoskote and nearby emerging areas have attracted attention because of their connectivity and development potential.

01

Kanakapura Road: A Blend of Connectivity and Lifestyle

Kanakapura Road has evolved from a primarily peripheral corridor into a more established residential and lifestyle destination. Its appeal comes from a combination of:

  • Connectivity to South Bangalore
  • Access towards NICE Road and surrounding corridors
  • Growing residential development
  • Educational and institutional presence
  • Access to green and leisure destinations

The corridor also offers opportunities for buyers looking beyond highly congested central areas.

02

Why Consider Kanakapura Road?

For land and plotted developments, the larger appeal lies in the possibility of combining urban accessibility with relatively open surroundings. As development progresses, carefully selected locations with good road access can become increasingly relevant for both end users and long-term investors.

03

STRR Hoskote: A Strategic Connectivity Corridor

The Satellite Town Ring Road (STRR) is intended to improve connectivity between satellite towns around Bengaluru and provide an alternative regional movement corridor.

NHAI’s project documents include the Dobbaspete–Hoskote section as an 80-km, six-lane Bangalore Ring Road project, while other STRR phases connect additional parts of the Bengaluru region. This type of infrastructure can be particularly significant for areas where road connectivity influences industrial, logistics, residential and commercial development.

04

Why Hoskote Attracts Attention

Hoskote is strategically positioned towards the eastern side of Bengaluru and has connectivity towards:

  • Whitefield
  • Bengaluru–Chennai corridor
  • Old Madras Road
  • Malur
  • Devanahalli and North Bangalore through regional road networks
  • Other parts of the STRR

The combination of highway connectivity and its position within the larger Bengaluru metropolitan region makes the Hoskote belt an important area to watch.

05

Industrial and Logistics Potential

Peripheral corridors with strong highway connectivity often become attractive for logistics, warehousing and industrial activity. As these ecosystems develop, supporting residential and commercial requirements can also emerge.

This doesn’t mean every property in the region will appreciate equally. The exact location, road frontage, access, zoning, approvals and surrounding development remain crucial.

06

Looking Beyond the City: The Opportunity in Emerging Corridors

The common factor connecting Kanakapura Road, STRR Hoskote and other peripheral Bengaluru corridors is the transition from peripheral locations to integrated growth zones.

For land investors, this transition can be important. However, infrastructure-led investment requires patience. Roads and proposed projects may take years to influence surrounding development, and announced projects can change in scope or timelines.

Therefore, investors should evaluate:

  1. Connectivity — How easily can the property be accessed from major roads and highways?
  2. Infrastructure — Which infrastructure projects are completed, under construction or merely proposed?
  3. Surrounding Development — Are residential, commercial, industrial or institutional developments actually coming up nearby?
  4. Approvals and Documentation — Always verify land title, conversion status, approvals, layout permissions and applicable development regulations.
  5. Developer Credentials — The track record and transparency of the developer or landowner should be carefully evaluated.
  6. Long-Term Potential — Land investment is generally better evaluated with a longer time horizon rather than through short-term expectations.
